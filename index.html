About the Guide

Welcome!

Welcome to our learning guide which aims to educate the public on pet rats, rat behaviour, and working rats. For a brief overview of those topics, read the information presented on the right side. From there, you can explore more in-depth material that we have categorized by format: journal articles, print books, videos, and websites.

To find out how we compiled these resources, look at our Search Strategies. Also, many of the keywords/key phrases we used are listed below. This learning guide is brought to you by Marie-Michele Chezzi and Michelle Wilson - feel free to contact us!

Audience

This learning guide is intended to both excite and educate children and adults on pet rat ownership. However, it is also intended to eradicate the negative stereotype that rats are simply annoying, disease-ridden animals. We did this by providing resources on the complexity of rat behaviour, the extent of the adoration of current rat owners, and the utility of rats as “working rats” that can help human society.

At a Glance

Topic Overview

Pet Rats

  • Intelligent, social companions that thrive living in pairs or small groups.
  • Need daily out-of-cage time, enrichment, and a properly sized, multi-level cage.
  • Bond closely with their owners and can be trained to do tricks.

Rat Behaviour

  • Highly food-motivated and quick to learn through positive reinforcement.
  • Communicate through body language, vocalizations, and scent-marking.
  • Naturally curious and playful, with a strong drive to explore and problem-solve.

Working Rats

  • Trained for scent-detection work such as landmine clearance and tuberculosis screening.
  • Organizations like APOPO train African giant pouched rats, known as “HeroRATs.”
  • Rely on the same trainability and food motivation that make rats such rewarding pets.
