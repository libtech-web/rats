How We Searched

Search Strategies

These are examples of the search strategies we used to find resources for this guide, along with the result each search produced. Depending on the medium used (e.g. Youtube, the Dicovery Layer, Academic Search Complete, etc), we employed different strategies. For example, with Academic Search Complete, we sometimes searched by subject headings and/or filtered by the type of source we were looking for. With Youtube we just did simple keyword searches with no boolean operators.

Discovery Layer

Langara's library Discovery Layer searches across multiple databases and collections at once, which made it a strong first stop for peer-reviewed journal articles on our topic.

Search working AND rats → Neville, Vikki, et al., “Identifying Personality Dimensions in Working African Giant Pouched Rats Using Caregiver Ratings”
Search pet AND rats → Walburn, Caitlin, et al., “Through Human Eyes: Owner Insights into the Social Relationships of Pet Rats”
Search TI(pet) AND TI(rat*) → Schneidewind, Stephanie, and Ines Windschnurer, “Associations between Husbandry Conditions, Human-Animal Interactions and Animal Behavior in Pet Rats”

Databases

For more targeted academic searching, we went directly into Langara's Academic Search Complete database. We used boolean operators, subject headings, and filters.

Search rodent AND driving → Crawford, L. E., et al., “Enriched Environment Exposure Accelerates Rodent Driving Skills”
Search rodent AND “tuberculosis detection” → “Heroes Come in All Shapes and Sizes, Including 3-Foot-Long Rodents”
Search “rat behavio*” → Reinhold, Annika Stefanie, et al., “Behavioral and Neural Correlates of Hide-and-Seek in Rats”
Search SU(pets) AND SU(rats) → “Rat Helps Hook School to the Internet”

YouTube

For creator-made videos on rat care and training, YouTube's own search box was the fastest way to find channels with real hands-on experience.

Search how to start training pet rat → “How to Start Training Your Rats (Updated)!”
Search fancy rat enclosure → “What Cages Are Best for Rats?”
Search fancy rat litter training → “How to Litter Train Rats!”
Search rat cage setup → “How to Setup a Rat Cage”

Public Library Catalogues

Print books came from several different library systems, each searched through its own catalogue or OPAC.

VPL Catalogue - Subject Search “rats as pets” → Toor, Rachel, Misunderstood: Why the Humble Rat May Be Your Best Pet Ever - Vancouver Public Library (Discovery Layer)
Langara Library Catalogue - Basic Search Subject rats → Rat City (CBC documentary) - Langara College Catalogue
BC Union Catalogue (Illume) - Subject Search rats as pets training → Bucsis, Gerry, Training Your Pet Rat
Vancouver Island Regional Library OPAC rodents → Munro, Roxie, Rodent Rascals - Discovery Layer

Websites

For blogs, rescue organizations, and general websites, we searched directly in Brave rather than a library tool, since this material typically lives outside library collections.

Search rat rescue Ontario → “Rat Care” (OPRats Ottawa Pet Rat Rescue)
Search caring guide AND pet rats → “A Guide to Keeping and Caring for Pet Rats” (The Spruce Pets)
Search fancy rats AND tricks → “Meet My (Clicker Trained) Pet Rats” (Stale Cheerios)
Search rat guide → “A Layman’s Guide to Health, Medication Use, Breeding, and Responsible Care of Pet Rats” (Rat Guide) - also found as a hyperlink on the Ottawa Rat Rescue website
Search rats AND “driving cars” → Lambert, Kelly, The Lab Rat Chronicles (original search; later re-found via a hyperlink in the University of Richmond’s Impact publication)
Search veterinarian AND pocket pets AND training → “What Is a Pocket Pet Vet?” (Vet Explains Pets)
